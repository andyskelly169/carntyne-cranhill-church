Who we are

About our church

Carntyne & Cranhill Parish Church is part of the Church of Scotland. We seek to be a welcoming Christian presence in our community, sharing God's love through worship, fellowship and practical care.

Our church is for people of all ages and backgrounds. You do not need to know anything about church to come along.

Come and join us

Worship with us

We would be delighted to welcome you to a Sunday service. Come as you are and join us for worship, prayer, reflection and fellowship.

For the latest service times and arrangements, please check our Facebook page or get in touch with the church.

Church is about more than Sunday worship. It is about friendship, encouragement, supporting our neighbours and being part of the life of our community.
